Good to know

How do I enroll?

Just call us, send an e-mail or use the online registration with MAXI. We take care of the rest.

At what age can I start?

With accompanied driving (BF17) you can apply from age 16½ and drive at 17. The regular minimum age is 18.

Do I learn with manual or automatic?

Both are possible. You decide what suits you best. We are happy to advise you.

How long does the training take?

That depends on you: our intensive course covers the theory in one week, and we schedule the practical lessons flexibly.
