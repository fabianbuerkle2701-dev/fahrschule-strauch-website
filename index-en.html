About us

Welcome to Fahrschule Viktor Strauch

We prepare you for your exam with modern, interactive lessons – using technology and hands-on practice instead of rote learning.

Our instructors love their job and support you calmly and without pressure. Mistakes are part of learning – with us you can always ask questions and progress at your own pace.
